From the editor — C.

Quieter week than last. Three of these came out of Boomkat's Tuesday rotation, the Klein record from a single Quietus paragraph that wouldn't leave me alone, and the Yu Su from a Bandcamp tag-walk that started with your dub-techno annotations from March. The throughline this week, if there is one, is patience — none of these records show their hand on a first listen, which is partly why they made the cut.

01 The lead
Hyperdub · HDB-098 2026 · 12″ EP
The lead record · via Boomkat · 4 May
Loraine James

Glitch the System

A bedroom-electronic record that finally lets the rhythm break the room.

Builds on the bedroom-electronic vulnerability of Building Something Beautiful For Me but pushes harder into rhythmic abstraction. Cut-up vocals collapse into half-formed phrases, kick drums arrive late and leave early, and the whole thing has the quality of being half-remembered — a record that sounds like it's already part of your past on first listen.

this is where that lineage is right now

You flagged Hyperdub-adjacent stuff in your seed back in February — Burial, Ikonika, the Hardcore Continuum reissues. James has been the most interesting artist on that label for three records running, and this one is the first where the song-craft and the texture finally pull in the same direction. Recommended loud, on headphones, walking somewhere.

Aquarium Drunkard · 2 May
Yu Su

Melaleuca

If the Tim Hecker entry on your seed was your way of asking for ambient that still has bones, this is it. Dub-inflected, patient, the percussion sits low in the mix like a heartbeat through a wall. Music for the second cup of coffee, not the first.

▸ Listen
03
Resident Advisor · 5 May
upsammy

Resort to Mountains

Dutch producer who came up through Nous'klaer; this new one for AD 93 abandons the kosmische steady-state for something more ruptured. The arpeggios are still there, but they're being interrupted now. Recommended if you've been wanting Caterina Barbieri but with more weather.

▸ Listen
04
The Quietus · 6 May
Klein

Whether

Vocal collage and broken Logic plugins; Klein's been doing this for years but this one finally has the song-craft to match the texture. Track 3 is the one — the chord changes pull from gospel and then refuse to land. If "pulls from gospel and then refuses to land" isn't your phrase yet, it will be.
